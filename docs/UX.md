# Designed for the people who use it

An organisational survey is not a one-off form: it is a small process that
moves between four kinds of people. The interface is built around **what each
of them is trying to get done**.

## Everyone

- **"What do I need to do?"** The sidebar shows a badge on *My tasks*; the
  dashboard's first card is *Waiting on you*; each task says what is needed
  (Respond, Revise and resubmit, Review, Approve) and how urgent it is.
- **Deadlines are visible, not hidden in a column.** Overdue surveys show a red
  badge ("Overdue 3 days"), ones due within three days show amber, in the lists,
  the tasks inbox, the dashboard and the survey page itself.
- **"Who is holding this up?"** Every list has a *Waiting on* column: the person
  it is with, their role, and how long it has been there.
- **Works on a phone.** Managers answer and approve between meetings, so the sidebar
  becomes a drawer and tables scroll within themselves.
- **Light and dark themes**, keyboard-friendly dialogs, labelled controls.

## Responders

- **Long surveys are safe to do over several sittings.** Answers are saved as a
  private draft a moment after each change, and restored on return. The reviewer
  sees nothing until you submit.
- A **progress bar** ("3 of 5 required answered").
- Submitting early **highlights every missed question** and jumps to the first.
- **Submitting asks for confirmation**, naming who it goes to.
- A returned survey opens with your previous answers and the reviewer's comment on top.

## Reviewers and approvers

- The response is shown in the survey's own sections, with the activity log beside it.
- Returning always needs a comment, so the responder knows what to fix.
- **Export CSV** of the response, for filing or analysis.

## Administrators

- **Suggest from the org chart**: the head of the block the survey is aimed at
  responds, the head one level up reviews, the head above approves. One click fills all three.
- **Dashboard cards link to the filtered list** (Overdue, In progress, Returned, Drafts), and
  *Needs attention* lists overdue surveys with who is holding each up.
- **Org chart for a large organisation**: fold and unfold branches, *Collapse all* /
  *Expand all*, **find a block by name**, zoom (buttons or Ctrl + wheel). Big organisations open folded.
- After publishing, **swap a person or move the deadline** without disturbing the questions.
- **Duplicate** a survey to run it again next quarter; **Close** one that is no longer needed.
- Reports **export to CSV**.

## Guard rails

- Unsaved changes (the survey builder, a response in progress) trigger the browser's
  "leave this page?" warning.
- Destructive actions (delete, close, publish, submit) always ask first and say what will happen.
- Errors say what to do ("Add a comment explaining what to fix"), never just "invalid".
- A person can't be given two roles on one survey; the pickers grey out anyone already chosen.

## Not built yet (worth knowing)

- **Email or in-app notifications** when a task arrives. Today people find tasks in the app
  (badge, dashboard, *My tasks*).
- **Forgot-password by email.** An administrator can set a new password from *Users*.
- **Anonymous responses**, recurring schedules, and reminders.
